A two-part micro-confession: a To: line naming who it was meant for, and
the unsent line itself, one line written exactly as it was typed.

Entry (143 characters):

> To: Grandma, who taught me to write letters
> I finally wrote you the letter you always asked for, and I finished it the week after your funeral.

`main.py` writes the entry to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`),
UTF-8, no trailing newline.

Why this one: the To: line is load-bearing — the letter only exists
because Grandma taught letters, so swapping the addressee breaks the
whole entry. The reason it stayed in drafts is felt, never stated: the
letter was finished a week too late. Specific, honest, and never sent.
